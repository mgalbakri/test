# Change note - master bedroom joinery re-measure

Quotation R1-M.A.740-26 - issued 2026-09-18

**For the contractor to re-quote.** Owner instruction D-02.

## Why

Items 31 and 33 are both quoted at W=4260 mm. Measured against the as-built drawing, neither can be installed at that length. The BOQ already anticipates re-measurement: *"The project is re-measured for all items Excepet some of the items mentioned above the B.O.Q."*

The room itself is an ASSUMED identification (conflict C-03, decision D-01). If the owner's mark-up places the master bedroom in a different space, this note is void and the quantities must be re-taken.

The pro-rata column is **indicative only**. Joinery does not price linearly - carcass, ironmongery, mouldings and fitting carry fixed cost - so the contractor's re-quote will not match it. It is given so the order of magnitude is visible before the re-quote arrives.

## Detail

**R1-M.A.740-26/31 - Wardrobe run, master bedroom**

- Quoted 4260 mm; installable 2630 mm.
- Quoted W=4260 mm. The wardrobe recess measures 2630 x 480 mm clear, so a 4260 mm run cannot be installed.

**R1-M.A.740-26/33 - TV-area run, master bedroom**

- Quoted 4260 mm; installable 3140 mm.
- Quoted W=4260 mm. The facing wall is 4260 mm long but carries a 1000 mm window, leaving 3260 mm in two pieces. 120 mm is held clear of the headboard, so 3140 mm is installable, as 1450 + 1690 mm.

## What this note does not change

- Item R1-M.A.740-26/32 (headboard, L=2600 mm) fits the measured wall exactly. No variance, no change.
- Door-leaf panels on items 31 and 33 stay unpriced and Phase 2 - *"the panel for door leaf will be wall paper or fabric the price will be separate"*. They are modelled as PH2 placeholders.
- Depth and height are unchanged: D=500 mm (the recess measures 480 mm, so the run sits 20 mm proud) and H=3000 mm floor-to-ceiling.
